# DO 视觉概念图 V2

## 核心方向

不是“一个带背景的 App 首页”，而是一张可以让人停留的生活照片：用户打开 DO，先进入一个安静、真实、有光线方向的私人空间；功能文字退到光影之后。

## 推荐生成提示词（UI 概念图）

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: mobile-first H5 app visual concept, portrait 9:16
Primary request: Design a premium immersive mobile interface for a product called DO. The product turns a vague thought into one tiny action, then keeps the lived moment as a personal journal page. Do not design a conventional social app, dashboard, todo list, card grid, or bottom navigation bar.
Scene/backdrop: A quiet sunlit desk beside a tall window, late afternoon. The camera feels like a beautiful editorial photograph taken from slightly above the desk. A warm beam of sunlight cuts diagonally across a dark walnut desktop. Dust particles are visible only inside the beam. Background is soft and out of focus.
Subject: In the center foreground, a small circular ivory object engraved with the word “DO”, like a refined paper seal or smooth stone, resting on the desk. It is the only obvious interactive object. Around it, a faint translucent input field appears to unfold from the circle, as if a note is being opened.
Composition/framing: Strong foreground / middle ground / background separation. Large calm negative space. The DO object sits slightly below center, not centered like a generic hero button. One short sentence floats above it with generous typography. A single narrow “recent page” glimpse is tucked at the bottom edge, like the corner of a photograph, not a navigation bar.
Lighting/mood: Directional golden sunlight, deep brown shadows, subtle reflected light, tactile and cinematic, calm but emotionally arresting, intimate, healing, premium editorial art direction.
Color palette: charcoal black, espresso brown, warm ivory, muted amber, faded olive. No neon gradients, no bright blue, no candy colors.
Materials/textures: walnut grain, matte paper, soft glass, linen, gentle film grain, realistic shadows, restrained blur.
Typography: elegant high-contrast serif for the emotional sentence, quiet neutral sans-serif for metadata. Chinese interface copy should feel literary and sparse. Use exact text: “把想做的事，放到今天。” and “我想……”
Interaction cue: Show one cohesive state transition in the same image sequence: the DO circle subtly opens, the input field grows from its center, and the surrounding light shifts slightly. The interaction should feel physical and soft, never like a modal popup.
Constraints: premium, minimal, coherent, one visual world, believable mobile H5 composition, no conventional app chrome.
Avoid: dashboard layout, multiple cards, bottom tab bar, generic purple gradient, floating glassmorphism panels, excessive rounded rectangles, random stock-photo background, loud icons, social metrics, likes, comments, followers, dense text, fake device frame, watermark.

## 可选第二张：翻开记录

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: mobile app interaction concept, portrait 9:16
Primary request: Show the same DO visual world after an action has happened. The screen feels like turning one page of a private life magazine. A real, ordinary moment is preserved with dignity: a pair of sneakers beside the desk, a glass of water, slightly rumpled clothing, evening light. The record is not a productivity achievement screen.
Composition/framing: A photograph-like page occupies most of the screen, with a quiet caption and time. The transition is implied by a translucent paper edge sliding across the frame, not a literal scrapbook UI. Keep one small DO mark as a visual continuity anchor.
Lighting/mood: honest, warm, imperfect, intimate, quietly beautiful.
Avoid: streaks, badges, checkmarks, achievement language, social feed, engagement buttons, collage clutter.
